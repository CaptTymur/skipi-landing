Pricing

Skipi is free for seafarers. The professional contours — Broker and Crewing — are paid, per seat, per month.

Availability. Broker and Crewing are sold through Paddle. Checkout opens when our Paddle account is live; the prices and terms on this page are the terms we sell on from that date.

Plans

Skipi Seafarer — free. The desktop and mobile app for seafarers: documents, certificates, packages, jobs. No payment details required.

Skipi Assistant — free. The maritime AI assistant at assistant.skipi.app.

Skipi Broker — $10 per seat, per month. Includes a 14-day free trial.

Skipi Crewing — $10 per seat, per month. Includes a 14-day free trial.

What a seat is

One seat is one named person, tied to the email address it is registered to. A company can hold several seats; each seat is paid for separately. Seats are not shared between people.

Billing

Paid plans are billed monthly in advance, in US dollars, and renew automatically until you cancel. You can cancel at any time.

Free trial. Broker and Crewing include a 14-day free trial. If you cancel before the trial ends, you are not charged.

Price changes. The prices above are our current prices. We may change prices, or the fair-use limits below, in future. Any change applies from your next renewal only, and we will email you at least 30 days before it takes effect. We never reprice a period you have already paid for. We do not sell lifetime or perpetual licences.

Fair use. A subscription includes AI assistance for normal individual professional use. Automated, resold or shared use can cost many times more to serve than a seat. If your usage is materially above typical, we will contact you and agree limits or a suitable plan with you — we will not restrict a paid subscription without contacting you first.

Taxes. Prices are shown excluding VAT and sales tax. Where tax applies, it is calculated and added at checkout based on your location, and shown on your invoice.

Who you buy from

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle handles payment, invoicing and applicable taxes, and appears on your bank statement.

Refunds

14 days, full refund, no questions asked. See Refunds and cancellation.
