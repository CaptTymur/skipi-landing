Delete your Skipi account

One Skipi account is used by all Skipi products: the Skipi Seafarer app on Google Play and the App Store, Skipi Assistant at assistant.skipi.app, Skipi Crewing and Skipi Broker. Deleting the account closes it for all of them.

There are two ways to ask for deletion. Below them is the honest list of what is deleted, and what is kept and why.

1. In the app — Skipi Seafarer

Settings → “Skipi account” → “Delete account…”

Confirm with your account password. The whole path runs inside the app and does not send you to a browser. Before you confirm, the screen shows what is deleted and what is kept — the same two lists as on this page.

The item is in Skipi Seafarer 0.4.190 and later. If your copy of the app is older and has no such item, update the app or use the email route below.

2. By email — if the app is already removed

Write to info@skipi.app from the address your account is registered to.

  • Subject: Delete my Skipi account
  • In the message: the email address the account is registered to.

We answer within two business days, and we write to you again when the account is deleted. We ask you to send the request from the registered address so that nobody can close an account that is not theirs; if you no longer have access to that address, say so and we will agree another way to check that the account is yours.

What is deleted

  • your sign-in data: display name, email address and password;
  • your seafarer profile as it is stored in your Skipi account;
  • the files kept for your account on our servers — your account vault folder and the files attached to packages you have sent;
  • your assistant conversations stored for the account, including questions still waiting in the queue and the answers to them;
  • service emails to you that are still in our sending queue, and any sign-in links issued for your address;
  • the remaining records tied to your account in the service: mailings, packages, messages and their attachments.

Deletion runs at the moment you confirm it — there is no waiting period, and your sign-in stops working straight away.

What is kept, and why

  • Payment records, anonymised. The amount, date, reference code and status of each payment stay in our books, without your name or contact details. A seller has to keep records of what was sold and invoiced; UK GDPR Article 17(3)(b) is the ground for keeping them.
  • An empty account row. The account itself stays as an anonymised marker — no name, no password, no working sign-in — because the payment records above refer to it. Nobody can log into it, and it holds nothing about you beyond that reference.
  • Working logs of the assistant service. While the assistant answers questions it writes working logs, and account deletion does not reach them today. If you want the entries about you removed from those logs, say so in your email and we do it by hand. We would rather write this down than promise a clean sweep we cannot perform.
  • Operational backups. We keep backups of our databases so the service can be restored after a failure. A backup taken before your deletion still holds what was in the database at that moment, until it is replaced by a newer one. Backups are not used to bring deleted accounts back.
  • Anything already delivered to someone else. A CV, package or message that has reached an employer, a crewing company or another recipient is in their hands, and we cannot recall it.

Documents on your device

Skipi keeps your vault on your own device. Deleting the account does not touch it, and we cannot touch it either — those files are not on our servers unless you send them somewhere. If you want them gone from the device, delete the vault folder, and the app, yourself.

If you pay for a subscription

Cancel the subscription before you delete the account. Deleting the account does not cancel it: the subscription is held by Paddle, our merchant of record, and it keeps renewing until it is cancelled. Use the cancellation link in your Paddle receipt email, or write to info@skipi.app and we cancel it for you. Details: Refunds and cancellation.

Questions

Anything unclear about deletion — write to info@skipi.app and a person answers.
