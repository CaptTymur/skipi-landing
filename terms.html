1. What Skipi Is

Skipi is a local-first maritime document and communication tool. Depending on the component you use, it may provide:

  • a local desktop vault for seafarer documents, contracts, sea service records, CV generation, and document packages;
  • a public vacancy board that seafarer desktop apps may poll when the user enables job alerts;
  • encrypted message relay between a seafarer and a verified employer-side Skipi Crewing user;
  • employer-side tools for verified crewing or maritime companies to publish vacancies and receive communications.
