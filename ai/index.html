Skipi connector

Skipi for Claude and ChatGPT

Connect your Skipi account to the AI assistant you already use. It can search Skipi vacancies and read your own seafarer data. Read-only.

What the connector does

The Skipi connector is a remote MCP server at https://mcp.skipi.app/mcp. Once you connect it and sign in with your Skipi account, your assistant gets four read-only tools:

  • Search Skipi — find vacancies and other public records on the Skipi board.
  • Fetch — open one record from the search results in full.
  • My Skipi profile, documents and sea service — your rank, availability, nationality, visas, languages, preferred vessel types and expected salary; the list of your documents with validity dates and expiry status; your sea service history.
  • Connection status — how the connector is connected.

The connector cannot change anything in your account. It never returns document numbers, phone numbers, your address, next-of-kin details or the document files themselves.

What you need

A Skipi account with the seafarer role (free — register at assistant.skipi.app or in the Skipi Seafarer app) and an account with Claude or ChatGPT.

Connect in Claude

  1. In Claude open Customize → Connectors, press + and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Enter the address https://mcp.skipi.app/mcp and press Add, then Connect.
  3. A Skipi page opens: sign in with your Skipi email and password and press Allow.

Connect in ChatGPT

  1. In ChatGPT open the apps section and choose Add custom MCP server.
  2. Enter the address https://mcp.skipi.app/mcp, authentication: OAuth.
  3. Sign in with your Skipi email and password and press Allow.

In Claude Code: claude mcp add --transport http skipi https://mcp.skipi.app/mcp, then /mcp and sign in.

Things to ask

  • “Which of my documents expire in the next six months?”
  • “Find vacancies on Skipi that match my rank and vessel type.”
  • “Summarise my sea service for a cover letter.”

Your data and how to disconnect

When you press Allow, the data listed above is sent to the assistant service you chose (Anthropic for Claude, OpenAI for ChatGPT) each time the assistant uses a Skipi tool, and is handled by that service under its own terms. Skipi sends nothing until you connect, and only for your own account.

To disconnect, open your Skipi account at assistant.skipi.app → Linked devices and revoke the entry marked “AI assistant (read-only)”, or remove the connector in your assistant. Access stops immediately. A connection that is not used for 90 days expires by itself.
