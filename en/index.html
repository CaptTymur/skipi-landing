How it works

From the seafarer's document to the crewing decision

Three steps in one loop. The seafarer maintains the vault, the crewing receives the data, nothing gets lost in email threads.

1

Profile and vault

The seafarer creates a local vault, scans certificates — AI extracts expiry and issuing authority. Structure follows rank and STCW.

2

Package and application

For a vacancy or a crewing request, a package is built: CV and the relevant certificates. Skipi verifies nothing is expired before sending.

3

Structured intake

The crewing receives a structured bundle — not "20 disconnected attachments" — filterable by certificate type and validity.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Skipi free?

Skipi Seafarer is in public early access — free to download. Skipi Crewing is invite-only via an authorisation token; early adopters can request a token via info@tymur.org.

Where are documents stored?

On your device. Skipi uses a local-first architecture: the vault lives in files on your machine. Cloud sync is opt-in and explicit.

Does the recipient need an account?

No. The recipient opens the package via the link. The sender sets the expiry and download limit — this simplifies vetting, PSC, and charterer workflows.

How do the salary indices work?

Skipi aggregates open vacancies from crewing agencies and ATS, normalises by rank and vessel type, and publishes the median in the index. The seafarer sees the actual market.

Which operating systems are supported?

The desktop client builds for Windows, macOS, and Linux. Skipi is also available on the web; mobile clients are on the 2026 roadmap.

I'm a crewing company — how do I get access?

Send a short note from your corporate domain to info@tymur.org. We'll issue a token and run a short onboarding — typically 20–30 minutes.

Ready to try Skipi?

Download the Seafarer client now, or request a Crewing token — early access is free, no subscription, no contract.
