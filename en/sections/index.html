What is Skipi

Skipi Sections

Skipi is an ecosystem for people who live by the sea: an AI assistant purpose-built for shipping, apps for the seafarer, the crewing team and the broker, and a shared layer of maritime data that ties them together.

A specialized AI purpose-built for shipping — not a generic chatbot. Created by Captain Tymur Rudov on the collective experience of seafarers and up-to-date maritime context.
